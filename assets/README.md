# Project Images & Assets

Place your diagrams, schematics, prototype photos, and dashboard screenshots here.

---

### 1. Final-Year Research Project (VLC Module)
- `vlc-block-diagram.png` (or `.jpg`) — System architecture or functional block diagram
- `vlc-hardware-photo.jpg` (or `.png`) — Photo of your assembled hardware prototype or lab test setup

---

### 2. SLT Digital Lab / R&D Internship
- `thingsboard-dashboard.png` (or `.jpg`) — Screenshots of your ThingsBoard monitoring dashboard (charts, cards, alarms)
- `slt-hardware-setup.jpg` (or `.png`) — Photos of your ESP32 hardware test setup or prototype boards

---

### How to display an image in your portfolio:
In `index.html`, find the corresponding placeholder in the project section and replace the `<div class="dropzone-content">...</div>` with:
```html
<img src="assets/vlc-block-diagram.png" alt="VLC System Block Diagram" style="max-width: 100%; border-radius: 6px;">
```
